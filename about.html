About Align & Restore

Care that meets you
where you are

A trusted MSK physiotherapy clinic in Nottingham, dedicated to helping people move better, recover faster, and live pain-free.

Our mission is simple: to restore movement, relieve pain, and promote long-term well-being through compassionate, evidence-based physiotherapy care.

We understand that every patient is unique. That's why we take a personalised, one-to-one approach, focusing on identifying the root cause of your pain rather than just treating the symptoms. Using a combination of manual therapy, exercise rehabilitation, and postural correction, we help you achieve lasting results and confidence in your movement.
