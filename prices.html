FAQ

Frequently asked questions

You do not need a GP referral to see us. As HCPC-registered physiotherapists, we are qualified to assess, diagnose, and treat your condition directly. The title "Physiotherapist" is protected by law, so you can be confident you're seeing a fully trained and accredited professional. We can also communicate with your GP or other healthcare providers if needed, with your consent.
We do offer home visits within reasonable distances from our clinic. We charge the time to travel to and from the home visit within our normal hourly structure.
We try our best to make sure our expert physio service is as affordable as possible. The cost of a session will depend on how long you're being seen and what service or treatment you may be having. See the price list above for the full breakdown of costs.
Please wear loose, comfortable clothing that allows you to move freely. Shorts are helpful if we need to assess your hips, knees, or ankles. If you prefer not to remove any outer clothing, just let us know, and we can always work around your comfort while still assessing and treating you effectively.
You can reach us by phone on 07586 572901, by email at info@alignrestore.co.uk, or via our contact form. We are always happy to answer your questions.
If you feel more comfortable bringing a friend or family member to your physiotherapy consultation, this is not a problem. We are there to help you, and you are the focus of our support. If having someone with you during your sessions would be helpful, that's absolutely fine.
