Conditions We Treat

Whatever's holding
you back, we can help

From everyday aches to post-surgical recovery, our physiotherapy is tailored to your body, your goals, and your pace.

Category 01

Musculoskeletal (MSK) Conditions

Orthopaedic issues affecting joints, muscles and nerves, assessed and treated at the root cause.

  • Back pain
  • Neck pain
  • Shoulder pain / frozen shoulder
  • Rotator cuff injuries
  • Hip & knee pain
  • Sciatica / nerve irritation
  • Tennis & golfer's elbow
  • Wrist and hand pain
  • Ankle sprains / foot pain
  • Osteoarthritis (all joints)
  • Repetitive strain injuries (RSI)
  • Postural and movement dysfunction

Category 02

Post-Surgery Rehabilitation

Structured, guided recovery plans designed for faster, safer healing after surgery.

  • Joint replacement surgeries
  • ACL reconstruction
  • Meniscus repair
  • Rotator cuff repair
  • Tendon repairs (Achilles, biceps, shoulder)
  • Spinal surgery rehabilitation
  • Fracture fixation recovery
  • Post-arthroscopy rehab
  • Post-trauma stiffness and weakness
  • General post-operative mobility issues

Category 03

Conditions Suitable for Home Visits

Professional physiotherapy in your own home, ideal for reduced mobility or long-standing pain.

  • Post-surgery rehab at home
  • Reduced mobility and difficulty walking
  • Elderly rehabilitation
  • Balance issues / fall prevention support
  • General de-conditioning / weakness
  • Chronic back or knee pain
  • Arthritis-related mobility problems
  • Long-term pain management at home
